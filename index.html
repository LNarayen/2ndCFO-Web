Advisor Insight — Q4 Review
"Can we afford to hire another team member?"
At current utilization you have ~180 unbilled hours/month. That's $27k/mo unrealized. A new hire fully-loaded runs ~$18k/mo. The math works — if demand holds.
"Why did our margin drop in October?"
Three factors: a $22k write-down on the Henderson project, a 3-day owner absence, and slower-than-usual contract renewals. October is recoverable — November projections look stronger.
