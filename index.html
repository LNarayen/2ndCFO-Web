Why Law Firms

Law firms have unique financial dynamics most advisors miss.

The combination of WIP, realization rates, attorney utilization, retainer management, and partner compensation creates a financial picture that generic advisors simply don't understand.

We speak your language.

Realization rates, billable hour targets, matter profitability, WIP aging, trust accounting — these aren't foreign concepts to us. We track what actually moves the needle for law firms.

We're built for your size.

5 to 20 attorneys is a specific inflection point. Big enough to need real financial strategy. Too small to afford a full-time CFO. That's exactly where we operate.

We don't replace your bookkeeper.

We work on top of your existing bookkeeping setup. No disruption, no vendor replacement. We take what you already have and transform it into strategic intelligence.

We're locally focused.

Based in the Seattle area. We understand the local legal market, its competitive dynamics, and the economic conditions that affect your firm's performance.

"Should we bring on another associate?"
At 76% utilization you have ~180 unbilled hours/month. That's $27k/mo unrealized. A new associate fully-loaded runs ~$18k/mo. The math works — if intake holds. Let's stress-test your pipeline.
"Why did our margin drop in October?"
Three factors: a $22k write-down on the Henderson project, a 3-day owner absence, and slower-than-usual contract renewals. October is recoverable — November projections look stronger.

WIP aging, realization rates, matter profitability, partner compensation — law firm finances have unique dynamics most advisors miss. We know them cold.

The Common Thread

Different industries.
Same financial blind spots.

Across every industry we serve, founders and owners tend to face the same challenges — and benefit from the same core solutions.

Revenue looks good on paper, but the bank account tells a different story. We build rolling forecasts so you're never caught off guard.
